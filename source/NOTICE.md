# Teaching scaffold and attribution

This scaffold is learning support for 6CC545 CW1. Student authors must acknowledge supplied scaffolding and distinguish their changes. It is not a sample final submission.

No third-party runtime source, executable or vendor cache is bundled. Weeks 1-4 use the Go standard library. Week 5 adds the version-pinned gRPC and Protocol Buffers runtimes in go.mod. Their upstream licence notices remain authoritative. The protocol contract is supplied teaching material; Go bindings are produced locally as a Week 5 preparation task.

Do not expose this unencrypted teaching service beyond loopback. A syntactically valid ID or SHA-256 string does not authenticate a sender or prove file contents have been transferred.
